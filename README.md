## 👋 About me

I'm **João Guilherme**, a **Senior iOS Developer** from **Manaus, Brazil 🇧🇷** with **6+ years** of experience building mobile apps — currently focused on **digital banking & fintech**.

- 📱 Building and maintaining iOS flows for a **digital bank**, with **Open Finance** (Brazilian Central Bank) payment journeys
- 🧩 Created a **white-label module** shared between two apps, cutting iOS development time by **~50%**
- 🚀 Raised deep-link payment flow conversion from **~28% to 80–90%**
- 🛠️ Modernized the iOS pipeline with **Fastlane**, **Firebase** and **Crashlytics**
- 🏆 **Apple Developer Academy** alumni and **WWDC award winner**
- 🎓 B.Sc. in **Software Engineering** — Federal University of Amazonas (UFAM)
- 🌍 Fluent in English (C1) · open to **remote & international** opportunities
- 📷 Off the keyboard: **analog photography**, **Pokémon** and **music**
